How the brief is built

  1. Scan the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and S&P 400 stocks, with their sectors and industry groups.
  2. Score the market from QQQ’s trend, breadth, risk appetite, volatility and rates, then set Regime A to E.
  3. Open or close the long, pilot and short gates; with a gate closed, its setups are listed for observation only.
  4. Grade every setup A or B with an 8-item checklist and work out an illustrative size from a fixed risk per unit.
  5. List no new setups just before earnings, and recalculate every parameter against pre-market prices before the open.

An automated morning workflow for US stocks: market regime, breadth and technical screening across the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and S&P 400, recalculated by fixed rules every trading day before the open.
